About Cricket Lite

Cricket Lite is a free, browser-based 2D cricket game in the Qelzavryn collection of web projects. It is designed for quick matches without an account or installation.

How to play

Play solo against the computer or share one device with a friend in local 1v1 mode. Choose a 2-, 5-, 10-, 20-, or 50-over match, bat and bowl, and try to win before your wickets or overs run out.
