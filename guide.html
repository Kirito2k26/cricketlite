CricketLite Game Guide

Batting practice

Start a two-over solo match on Easy. Choose straight and ground shot. Watch the red ball approach the striker and press Swing near contact. Compare the timing feedback across deliveries before attempting lofted shots.

Run when the ball is away from fielders; use Return to cancel a risky run. Lofted shots can be caught.

Bowling practice

Choose pace or spin and a delivery type. Tap or click the pitch to aim, then bowl. Aim near the stumps first; moving too far outside can result in wides. Try different field presets to see how they affect coverage.

Match rules

Each innings ends after the selected number of legal balls or ten wickets. Wides and no-balls add extras and do not count as legal deliveries. The chasing side wins when it exceeds the first innings score.
