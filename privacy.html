Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

This policy explains how Cricket Lite handles information when you visit and play at cricketlite.vercel.app.

Game data

Cricket Lite does not require an account. The game runs in your browser. It uses your browser's local storage to save a limited history of match results (up to 20 records). This information stays in your browser unless you choose to share it. You can clear it through your browser's site-data settings.

Hosting and technical information

The website is hosted on Vercel. As with most hosted websites, the hosting provider may process technical information such as IP addresses, request logs, browser details, and security-related data to deliver and protect the site. Refer to Vercel's privacy information for details.

Advertising and analytics

Cricket Lite's included game files do not currently integrate an advertising or analytics service. If those services are introduced, this policy should be updated to explain the relevant data processing and choices.

Third-party links

Links to other websites, including Qelzavryn, may have their own privacy policies.

Children's privacy

Cricket Lite does not ask players to create accounts or submit personal details. Please do not email sensitive personal information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the website changes. The latest version will appear on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, email contact.qelzavryn@gmail.com.
